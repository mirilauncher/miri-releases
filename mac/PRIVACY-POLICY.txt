Miri Launcher for Mac - Privacy Policy
======================================
Made by @9lo.l on Discord.
Applies to: Miri Launcher Beta Mac
Last updated: November 26, 2026

Short version
-------------
Miri keeps everything on your own Mac. It has no accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics,
no ads and no tracking. The only places Miri talks to on the internet are Roblox and GitHub (for
Miri's own updates), and only to do
the things listed below.

What Miri stores, and where
---------------------------
Your settings are saved in your own user folder, ~/.config/MiriLauncher:

  accounts.json     The names and Roblox user IDs of the accounts you added.
  settings.json     Your launcher options, FastFlags and visual choices.
  RobloxMods/       The visual files you picked (fonts, skies, cursors, emote wheels...).
  RobloxModsBackup/ Copies of Roblox's original files, so removing a mod puts them back.
  avatars/          Your accounts' Roblox profile pictures, so the menu can show them.
  background        A copy of your own background picture, if you chose one.
  crash.log         Only written if Miri crashes: the error message, for fixing the bug.
                    It contains no passwords or cookies and is never uploaded.

Your Roblox sign-in (the .ROBLOSECURITY session cookie) for each account is kept in your Mac's
login Keychain, as a "Miri Launcher" item. macOS only lets Miri itself read it. Miri never
writes it to a file or a log, never shows it, and never sends it anywhere except Roblox.

Miri never sees your Roblox password. You sign in with Roblox's own sign-in code: Miri shows
a code, and you approve it on roblox.com (or in the Roblox app) on a device where you're
already signed in.

What Miri sends over the internet
---------------------------------
Only these, and only when the feature is used:

  apis.roblox.com                    Gets a sign-in code and checks whether you approved it.
  auth.roblox.com                    Finishes the sign-in, and gets a one-time launch ticket
                                     when you press PLAY (the same way the Roblox website
                                     starts the game).
  users.roblox.com                   Checks which account a sign-in belongs to (name and ID).
  thumbnails.roblox.com              Downloads your accounts' profile pictures (sends user IDs).
  roblox.com/crossdevicelogin        Opened in your web browser if you press "Open roblox.com"
                                     while adding an account, to enter the code.
  roblox.com/download                Opened in your web browser if Roblox isn't installed.
  create.roblox.com                  Opened in your web browser if you press Studio and Roblox
                                     Studio isn't installed (so you can download it from Roblox).
  api.github.com, github.com         Checks Miri's own download page (github.com/mirilauncher/
                                     miri-releases) for a newer Miri when it opens, and downloads
                                     it. Sends nothing about you or your accounts.

Like any internet connection, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy
policies apply (Roblox: roblox.com/info/privacy, GitHub: docs.github.com/site-policy).

Miri does not send anything to its developer (@9lo.l on Discord) or to anyone else. There is no
telemetry, no usage statistics, no crash reporting and no automatic upload of any file.

Sharing
-------
Miri does not sell, share or rent any data, because it never collects any.

Children
--------
Miri collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Roblox's own rules and
age requirements still apply to your Roblox account.

Removing your data
------------------
- In Miri: remove an account in the Roblox tab to delete its saved sign-in from the Keychain.
- Visual mods Miri copied into Roblox can be removed from Settings > Visuals ("Remove all").
  A Roblox update or reinstall also removes them.
- Then drag "Miri Launcher" from Applications to the Bin, and delete the folder
  ~/.config/MiriLauncher (in Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., then type ~/.config).
