What Miri Launcher does (and why it's safe)
===========================================
Made by @9lo.l on Discord.
Miri Launcher Beta is a Roblox launcher for Windows 10/11. It lets you keep several Roblox
accounts, start Roblox with one click, and change how Roblox looks on your own PC.

What it does
------------
- Accounts: you sign in on Roblox's real login page. Miri saves the sign-in encrypted on your
  PC, so you can switch accounts and press PLAY without typing your password again.
- PLAY: asks Roblox for a one-time launch ticket and opens the Roblox app, the same way the
  Roblox website does.
- Studio: opens Roblox Studio if it's installed. Studio signs in by itself; Miri gives it
  nothing. If Studio isn't installed, Miri offers to open Roblox's Creator Hub to get it.
- Visuals: skies, fonts, cursors, emote wheels, textures and graphics settings. Miri copies
  picture/font files into Roblox's own folders on your PC (and into Bloxstrap, Froststrap,
  Fishstrap and similar launchers' "Modifications" folder). This is the same kind of file
  swap those launchers do. Roblox's original files are backed up and put back when you
  remove a visual.
- FastFlags: Roblox's own graphics and performance settings, written to Roblox's
  ClientAppSettings.json file. Roblox decides which ones it accepts.
- Keeping Roblox up to date: plain Roblox is updated from Roblox's own download servers,
  in the background.
- Keeping itself up to date: when Miri opens, it checks github.com/mirilauncher/miri-releases
  in the background. A newer Miri is downloaded and swapped in, and Miri asks if you want to
  restart now. Your accounts and settings stay as they are.

What it does NOT do
-------------------
- It is not a cheat or exploit. It never injects code into Roblox, never reads or changes
  Roblox's memory while it runs, and gives no advantage in games. Every change is a file on
  your PC that only changes what YOU see.
- It never sees your Roblox password and never sends your sign-in anywhere except Roblox.
- It has no ads, no tracking, no telemetry and no servers of its own. See PRIVACY-POLICY.txt.
- It doesn't need admin rights. Apart from Roblox itself, the only thing it may install is
  Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime (only if your PC doesn't have it; it's needed for sign-in).

What it changes on your PC
--------------------------
- Installs to %LocalAppData%\Programs\MiriLauncher, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts
  and an entry in Settings > Apps (uninstall from there any time).
- Saves its data in %AppData%\MiriLauncher (see PRIVACY-POLICY.txt for every file).
- Writes visual files into Roblox's folders, as described above.
- When it updates plain Roblox, it points Windows' "roblox-player" link at the new Roblox
  version, which is what Roblox's own updater does too.

"Windows protected your PC"
---------------------------
Windows shows this blue screen for any new program that isn't signed with a paid
code-signing certificate and hasn't been downloaded by many people yet. It doesn't mean
Windows found anything wrong with Miri. To run it: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Please only download Miri from the person or page you got it from originally. A copy from
anywhere else could have been changed.
