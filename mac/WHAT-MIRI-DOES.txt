What Miri Launcher does on a Mac (and why it's safe)
====================================================
Made by @9lo.l on Discord.
Miri Launcher Beta Mac is a Roblox launcher for macOS 12 or newer. It lets you keep several
Roblox accounts, start Roblox with one click, and change how Roblox looks on your own Mac.

What it does
------------
- Accounts: you add an account with Roblox's own sign-in code. Miri keeps the sign-in in your
  Mac's Keychain, so you can switch accounts and press PLAY without signing in again.
- PLAY: asks Roblox for a one-time launch ticket and opens the Roblox app, the same way the
  Roblox website does.
- Studio: opens Roblox Studio if it's installed. Studio signs in by itself; Miri gives it
  nothing. If Studio isn't installed, Miri offers to open Roblox's Creator Hub to get it.
- Visuals: skies, fonts, cursors, emote wheels, textures and graphics settings. Miri copies
  picture/font files into Roblox's own app (Roblox.app/Contents/Resources). Roblox's original
  files are backed up and put back when you remove a visual.
- FastFlags: Roblox's own graphics and performance settings, written to Roblox's
  ClientAppSettings.json file (Roblox.app/Contents/MacOS/ClientSettings). Roblox decides
  which ones it accepts.
- Roblox updates: Roblox updates itself on a Mac, and an update replaces the whole app, which
  removes your visuals. Miri notices and puts them back, and also applies them every time you
  press PLAY.
- Keeping itself up to date: when Miri opens, it checks github.com/mirilauncher/miri-releases
  in the background. A newer Miri is downloaded and swapped in, and Miri asks if you want to
  restart now. Your accounts and settings stay as they are. (This needs Miri in your
  Applications folder, not opened straight from Downloads.)

What it does NOT do
-------------------
- It is not a cheat or exploit. It never injects code into Roblox, never reads or changes
  Roblox's memory while it runs, and gives no advantage in games. Every change is a file on
  your Mac that only changes what YOU see.
- It never sees your Roblox password and never sends your sign-in anywhere except Roblox.
- It has no ads, no tracking, no telemetry and no servers of its own. See PRIVACY-POLICY.txt.
- It doesn't need an administrator password and installs nothing else.

What it changes on your Mac
---------------------------
- The app itself, wherever you put it (normally Applications).
- Its data in ~/.config/MiriLauncher (see PRIVACY-POLICY.txt for every file).
- One Keychain item per account, named "Miri Launcher".
- Visual files and ClientAppSettings.json inside Roblox.app, as described above.

The Windows straps (Bloxstrap, Froststrap, Fishstrap...) don't exist on Mac, so the Mac version
has no "Roblox launcher" module. Everything else from the Windows version is here.
