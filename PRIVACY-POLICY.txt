Miri Launcher - Privacy Policy
==============================
Made by @9lo.l on Discord.
Applies to: Miri Launcher Beta
Last updated: November 26, 2026

Short version
-------------
Miri keeps everything on your own PC. It has no accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics,
no ads and no tracking. The only places Miri talks to on the internet are Roblox, Microsoft
and GitHub (for Miri's own updates),
and only to do the things listed below.

What Miri stores, and where
---------------------------
Everything is saved in your own Windows user folder, %AppData%\MiriLauncher:

  accounts.json     The names and Roblox user IDs of the accounts you added.
  secrets\          Your Roblox sign-in (the .ROBLOSECURITY session cookie) for each account,
                    encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Only your Windows user on this PC can decrypt
                    it. Miri never writes it to a log, never shows it, and never sends it
                    anywhere except Roblox.
  settings.json     Your launcher options, FastFlags and visual choices.
  RobloxMods\       The visual files you picked (fonts, skies, cursors, emote wheels...).
  RobloxModsBackup\ Copies of Roblox's original files, so removing a mod puts them back.
  avatars\          Your accounts' Roblox profile pictures, so the menu can show them.
  background        A copy of your own background picture, if you chose one.
  crash.log         Only written if Miri crashes: the error message, for fixing the bug.
                    It contains no passwords or cookies and is never uploaded.

Your Roblox password is typed into Roblox's own login page (roblox.com), inside a sign-in
window that uses a throw-away browser profile. Miri never sees or saves your password.
That temporary profile is deleted when the sign-in window closes.

What Miri sends over the internet
---------------------------------
Only these, and only when the feature is used:

  roblox.com (login page)            You sign in to Roblox there, directly.
  users.roblox.com                   Checks which account a sign-in belongs to (name and ID).
  auth.roblox.com                    Gets a one-time launch ticket when you press PLAY, the same
                                     way the Roblox website starts the game.
  thumbnails.roblox.com              Downloads your accounts' profile pictures (sends user IDs).
  clientsettingscdn.roblox.com       Asks which Roblox version is newest (sends nothing personal).
  setup.rbxcdn.com                   Downloads Roblox updates from Roblox's own servers.
  roblox.com/download                Downloads Roblox's official installer if Roblox is missing.
  go.microsoft.com                   Downloads Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime if your PC lacks it.
  create.roblox.com                  Opened in your web browser if you press Studio and Roblox
                                     Studio isn't installed (so you can download it from Roblox).
  api.github.com, github.com         Checks Miri's own download page (github.com/mirilauncher/
                                     miri-releases) for a newer Miri when it opens, and downloads
                                     it. Sends nothing about you or your accounts.

Like any internet connection, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy
policies apply (Roblox: roblox.com/info/privacy, Microsoft: privacy.microsoft.com,
GitHub: docs.github.com/site-policy).

Miri does not send anything to its developer (@9lo.l on Discord) or to anyone else. There is no telemetry,
no usage statistics, no crash reporting and no automatic upload of any file.

Sharing
-------
Miri does not sell, share or rent any data, because it never collects any.

Children
--------
Miri collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Roblox's own rules and
age requirements still apply to your Roblox account.

Removing your data
------------------
- In Miri: remove an account in the Roblox tab to delete its saved sign-in.
- Uninstall Miri (Settings > Apps, or "Uninstall Miri Launcher" in the Start menu) and choose
  "Yes" when asked whether to delete your saved accounts and settings. Or delete the folder
  %AppData%\MiriLauncher yourself.
- Visual mods Miri copied into Roblox's folders can be removed from the Visuals module
  ("Remove all") before uninstalling; reinstalling Roblox also removes them.
